This repository started life as an example repo for hosting GitHub Pages for RMaDSD and is now used to host web content for modules. The website contained within this repository can be found at: [luke-emery.github.io](https://luke-emery.github.io).
 
## Hello World
I am Luke Emery, delivering Computer Vision (COMP63040) at University of Staffordshire.

![Headshot of Luke Emery](/assets/images/Luke.png)

Within the university, I am a part-time lecturer (currently scheduled to be on campus on Tuesday) and have designed and delivered a wide range of modules, from C++ for Game Engine Creation and C89/90 for embedded systems to CAD for Robotic Modelling and LabVIEW for Robotic systems.

Outside of the university, I am the founder of Bright Idea, a business developing technology for themed entertainment and visitor attractions; this includes the use of additive manufacturing, primarily Fused Filament Fabrication 3D Printing, and software development with particular interests in cross platform frameworks and C++. Throughout a decade of both operational and technological experience in themed entertainment, I have been fortunate to work on major themed attractions, including one of the largest virtual reality dark ride installations in the world, and have a passion for the integration of computing in a way which is coherrent with the world design of themed attractions.
